# FreeMap

FreeMap discovers free and buy-one-get-one offers, evaluates the evidence behind
them, and presents trustworthy current deals to people in the Seattle area.

## Language

**Candidate**:
A discovered claim that an offer exists. A candidate is not visible to users
until it satisfies the publication policy.
_Avoid_: Deal, result, lead

**Evidence**:
A dated source observation that supports a candidate, including where the claim
was found and the relevant source text.
_Avoid_: Proof, citation

**Published deal**:
A candidate that currently satisfies FreeMap's scope, evidence, freshness, and
location requirements and is visible to users.
_Avoid_: Candidate, listing

**Publication decision**:
The current accepted, pending, or rejected outcome of evaluating a candidate.
_Avoid_: Status, moderation state

**Source tier**:
The relationship a source has to an offer: official, community, editorial, or
aggregator.
_Avoid_: Trust score, source quality

**Corroboration**:
Matching evidence for the same candidate from independent sources.
_Avoid_: Duplicate, confirmation
